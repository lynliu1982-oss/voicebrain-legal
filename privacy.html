Privacy Policy

VoiceBrain · Effective date: November 22, 2026

1. Who we are

VoiceBrain ("the app") is developed and published by an independent developer. For any privacy question, contact lynliu1982@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

  • Recordings — the audio files you record are saved in the app's own storage on your device.
  • Transcripts, notes, key points, to-dos and diary entries — saved in a local database on your device.
  • AI request history (the prompt text sent and the answer returned) — also saved in that local database so you can review it.
  • Subscription state as answered by Apple, and the last weekly AI count our server reported — saved in the app's local preferences so the screen can show them without waiting for the network.

We do not operate a user account system. Your library is never uploaded to us as a whole.

3. When data leaves your device

The app only sends data off the device when you use a feature that needs it:

Speech recognition. Recording is transcribed on your device with Apple's on-device speech framework. The quick voice search on the home screen also uses Apple's speech recognition; if your device or language cannot run it on device, Apple may process that short clip on its servers under Apple's privacy policy. We never send audio to our own servers.

Device attestation and AI usage count. On first launch the app uses Apple's App Attest to generate a cryptographic key on your device and obtains a signed attestation from Apple; the private key never leaves the device. The key identifier and Apple's attestation are sent once to our server, which keeps only the key identifier and the public key to verify that later AI requests come from this device, and to count how many AI requests you make each week (100 per week, resetting Monday). None of this contains personal information, is derived from your Apple ID, or can be used to identify you. Our server keeps this record and the weekly counter until you ask us to delete it (see Retention below); old records are not cleared automatically. If Apple's attestation is unavailable (for example on a simulator), AI features stay off; all other features keep working.

AI answers, key points and diary. Only the text of the notes involved (never the audio) is sent over HTTPS to a server operated by the developer, which forwards it to our AI provider, DeepSeek, solely to produce the result you asked for. We do not store your text on that server after the request completes. DeepSeek's handling of API data is described in DeepSeek's privacy policy. We do not use your notes for advertising or to build a profile of you.

Purchases. Handled entirely by Apple on your device; we never see or store your payment details, and no purchase receipt is sent to our server. The app reads your current subscription directly from Apple (StoreKit) to decide whether to show the subscription prompt; that answer stays on your device.

AI features stay switched off until you agree to this sharing in the app, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings → AI data sharing. With it off, nothing is sent to DeepSeek.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your notes for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Retention and deletion

Your recordings, notes and AI request history stay on your device until you delete them in the app. Deleting a note removes its audio and text. Deleting the app removes everything the app stored, including your library and the local AI request history.

On our server we keep only: the device key identifier, Apple's public attestation for that key, and the weekly AI usage count. None of these contain your notes, and we hold no record of your purchases. They are not removed automatically; email us at the address below and we will delete the record for your device.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Your rights

Because your content lives on your device, you can access, export and delete it directly in the app, and you can withdraw the AI sharing consent at any time. For any request about data we may hold (for example a purchase record held by Apple), contact us at the address above.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
