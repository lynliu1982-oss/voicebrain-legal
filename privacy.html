Privacy Policy

VoiceBrain · Effective date: November 22, 2026

1. Who we are

VoiceBrain ("the app") is developed and published by an independent developer. For any privacy question, contact lynliu1982@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

  • Recordings — the audio you record is saved in the app's own storage on your device.
  • Transcripts, notes, key points, to-dos, diary entries — saved in a local database on your device.
  • AI request history — the prompt text sent and the answer returned are also kept in that local database so you can review them.
  • Subscription state and usage counters — saved in the app's local preferences.

We do not run a user account system. Your library is never uploaded to us as a whole.

3. When data leaves your device

Speech recognition. Recording is transcribed on your device with Apple's on-device speech framework. The quick voice search on the home screen also uses Apple's speech recognition; if your device or language cannot run it on device, Apple may process that short clip on its servers under Apple's privacy policy. We never send audio to our own servers.

AI answers, key points and diary. Only the text of the notes involved (never the audio) is sent over HTTPS to a server operated by the developer, which forwards it to our AI provider, DeepSeek, solely to produce the result you asked for. We do not store your text on that server after the request completes. DeepSeek's handling of API data is described in DeepSeek's privacy policy. We do not use your notes for advertising or to build a profile of you.

Purchases. Handled by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Apple tells us only whether a subscription is active.

AI features stay switched off until you agree to this sharing in the app, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings → AI data sharing. With it off, nothing is sent to DeepSeek.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your notes for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Retention and deletion

Recordings, notes and AI request history stay on your device until you delete them in the app. Deleting a note removes its audio and text. Deleting the app removes everything the app stored, including your library and the local AI request history.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Your rights

Because your content lives on your device, you can access, export and delete it directly in the app, and you can withdraw the AI sharing consent at any time. For any request about data we may hold (for example a purchase record held by Apple), contact us at the address above.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.
