Privacy Policy

VoiceBrain · Effective date: November 21, 2026

1. Who we are

VoiceBrain ("the app") is developed and published by an independent developer. For any privacy question, contact lynliu1982@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

  • Recordings — the audio you record is saved in the app's own storage on your device.
  • Transcripts, notes, key points, to-dos, diary entries — saved in a local database on your device.
  • Subscription state and usage counters — saved in the app's local preferences.

We do not run a user account system, and we do not upload your library to our own servers.

3. When data leaves your device

Speech recognition. Audio is passed to Apple's on-device speech framework on iOS 26 and later. On devices that cannot use that framework, recognition runs locally with an offline model bundled in the app. No audio is uploaded for transcription in either case.

AI answers, key points and diary writing. The text of your notes (not the audio) is sent over HTTPS to a third-party large language model provider strictly to produce the result you asked for. Those requests are transient and are not used by us to build a profile of you.

Purchases. Handled by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Apple tells us only whether a subscription is active.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your notes for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Retention and deletion

Recordings and notes stay on your device until you delete them in the app. Deleting a note removes its audio and text. Deleting the app removes everything the app stored, including your library.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Your rights

Because your content lives on your device, you can access, export and delete it directly in the app. For any request about data we may hold (for example a purchase record held by Apple), contact us at the address above.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.
